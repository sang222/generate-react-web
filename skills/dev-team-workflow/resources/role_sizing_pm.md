# PM Sizing Guidance
- Level 0-1: write a compact scope, keep only essential user outcomes, and avoid over-specifying architecture.
- Level 2: define a normal V1 story with clear user flow, acceptance criteria, and realistic non-functional notes.
- Level 3-4: explicitly separate business goal, story boundaries, dependencies, protected areas, and delivery risks.
- For existing_project stories, emphasize change scope, out-of-scope boundaries, and regression-sensitive areas.
