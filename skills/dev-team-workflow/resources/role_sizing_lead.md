# Lead Sizing Guidance
- Level 0-1: keep the decision focused on whether the story is usable and safe to deliver.
- Level 2: weigh buildability, story completeness, and major QA blockers.
- Level 3-4: pay extra attention to baseline safety, integration quality, change scope discipline, and open risk items.
- For existing_project stories, prefer retry over delivery when protected or regression-sensitive areas are uncertain.
